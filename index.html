FAQ

Frequently asked questions

Learn more about this Nexora interface and its demonstration features.

What is Nexora?

Nexora is a fictional SaaS analytics platform concept created to demonstrate a modern landing page and interactive dashboard interface.

Does the dashboard use real business data?

No. The metrics, projects, chart values, and activity records are sample data for the frontend demonstration.

Can I switch between light and dark themes?

Yes. Use the theme buttons in the navigation or dashboard to switch the page appearance.

Do the pricing buttons process payments?

No. They demonstrate plan selection only. A payment provider and backend would be required for real subscriptions.

Can this interface be connected to a backend?

Yes. A future backend could provide authentication, persistent settings, real analytics, project storage, team management, and payment processing.
